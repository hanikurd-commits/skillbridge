# SkillBridge Europe

SkillBridge is a decision layer for cross-border work in Europe.

## Product thesis

Do **not** compete head-on with EURES (jobs/mobility), Europass (skills/CV/documents), or national recognition portals. The product should answer the earlier decision question:

> **Given my nationality/status, where I live, where my qualification comes from, my profession, experience and language, what is my realistic route to work in country X?**

The MVP therefore separates four questions:
1. Work/residence rights.
2. Professional qualification recognition.
3. Language / registration requirements.
4. Documents + next official action.

It also surfaces practical country-specific checks that people often discover late (pension, social insurance, digital government access, etc.).

## Market evidence

- EURES says Europe has major labour mismatches and that qualification recognition, language and lack of awareness block mobility.
- The European Commission reports migrants face language, recognition and administrative barriers and are often overqualified.
- The EU Talent Pool is being built to match non-EU jobseekers with shortage occupations; therefore SkillBridge should not be another generic job board.
- Official EU/German/Dutch/Belgian/French portals already contain large amounts of information. The opportunity is to make that information **personalised, sequential and source-backed**.

## Initial target users

1. Skilled workers moving from non-EU countries into Europe.
2. EU workers considering a move to another EU country.
3. Workers whose third-country qualification was recognised in one EU country and who later want to move again.

## Initial professions

Electrician, plumber, carpenter, welder, bricklayer, roofer, nurse, care worker, heavy truck driver, engineer, software developer and selected hospitality roles.

## Business model hypothesis

Free route screening → paid personalised route report / document-readiness pack → optional alerts and job-route matching → later B2B recruiter/employer tools.

The first monetisation test should be a low-priced personalised route report, not a paywall around basic official information.

## Next build order

1. Replace heuristic outputs with a real evidence-backed rules dataset.
2. Add profession + destination + qualification-country rules from authoritative sources.
3. Add language-specific requirements by profession where official sources provide them.
4. Add document readiness and missing-document warnings.
5. Add comparison of 3-5 target countries for one worker profile.
6. Add saved profiles and alerts after the decision engine is trustworthy.
7. Add jobs only as a downstream action, using official/public sources rather than building another generic board.

## Trust / legal rule

SkillBridge is not a law firm or immigration adviser. Important claims must link to official/authoritative sources, show a review date, and distinguish facts from screening heuristics. No numeric "eligibility score" should be presented as a legal determination.
